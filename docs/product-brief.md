# Product brief

## Problem

Conventional backsplash changes are messy, permanent, and labor-intensive. Renters, homeowners, and designers may want to refresh a surface often, replace a damaged tile, or remove the finish for deep cleaning.

## Proposed product

A two-layer system:

- **Permanent mounting grid:** establishes the tile pitch, displays the grout lines, and contains the attachment features.
- **Removable tile cartridges:** decorative faces mounted to carriers with top hooks, lower locking features, and a deliberate release path.

## Initial target user

DIY homeowners and makers who can install a small, mechanically fastened test panel and are comfortable inspecting prototypes. The first product should not target unattended commercial kitchens or high-heat zones.

## MVP boundaries

The MVP is a small wall panel, not a full kitchen installation. It should demonstrate 3 × 6 inch subway proportions, hand removal, replaceable latch parts, grout continuity, and repeatable fit.

## Success criteria

- One person can install and remove a tile without tools or damage.
- Tiles align consistently across a panel.
- A tile can be replaced without removing neighbors.
- The grid stays attached during normal hand operation.
- No sharp edges, pinch hazards, or inaccessible residue traps are found in inspection.
- A documented cycle test establishes a credible replacement interval for the latch.
