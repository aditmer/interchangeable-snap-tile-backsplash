# Interchangeable Snap-Tile Backsplash

An exploratory, tool-free backsplash system with a permanent mounting grid and removable decorative subway-tile cartridges.

## The concept

Install the alignment and attachment grid once. Snap individual decorative tiles on and off by hand, so a kitchen or utility space can change appearance without demolition, adhesive residue, or a toolbox.

```text
decorative tile face + carrier
          ↓ hook / press / click
mounting grid with visible grout
          ↓ screw or approved adhesive
wall substrate
```

The first prototype should prioritize safe removal, repeatable alignment, cleanability, and measurable cycle life over decorative complexity.

## Status

Concept and documentation phase. No part is approved for installation near heat, water, food preparation, or electrical equipment until the safety and cleanability tests in the plan are passed.

## Design principles

1. The wall-mounted grid owns alignment and grout appearance.
2. Decorative cartridges should be replaceable without replacing the grid.
3. Attachment force should be carried by durable geometry; flexible clips should be replaceable.
4. Water, grease, and cleaner must not be trapped in inaccessible cavities.
5. Every design claim should become a measurable test.
