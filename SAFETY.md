# Safety and responsible use

This repository documents prototypes, not a certified building product.

Do not install an untested prototype behind a cooktop, beside an open flame, in a commercial food-preparation area, or where local building, electrical, plumbing, fire, or landlord rules require approved materials.

Before any real installation, verify:

- substrate condition, fastener selection, and load path;
- compatibility of every material with heat, moisture, cleaners, and food-contact-adjacent use;
- required clearances from appliances and outlets;
- whether the chosen adhesive or fastener is approved for the substrate;
- that the installation can be removed without exposing hazards or damaging utilities.

Printed plastics may soften, creep, discolor, or release odors under heat. Prototype parts must be inspected for cracks, loose latches, sharp edges, warping, and retained moisture before every use. A failed latch should be treated as a replacement condition, not repaired with an improvised sharp or toxic part.

No material in this project should be represented as food-safe, flame-retardant, waterproof, or code-compliant without product-specific evidence and appropriate testing.
